Why it matters

Hospitals need dynamic disaster training, not passive scenario reading.

Infectious disease disasters place healthcare institutions under severe operational pressure. Teams must rapidly coordinate staffing, convert ward functions, manage infection risks, and maintain care continuity while conditions continue to change.

🏥

Operational Pressure

Hospitals must respond to sudden changes in patient volume, staff availability, workload, and infection risk.

👥

Team Coordination

Effective response requires coordinated ward-level decisions across nursing teams, managers, and emergency response units.

📊

Measurable Learning

Training outcomes should be captured, analyzed, and translated into structured feedback for improvement.

About the game

A simulation-based training platform for pandemic response.

The Golden Hour: Pandemic provides a realistic training environment where players make time-sensitive decisions on ward operation, nurse staffing, infection control, and resource allocation. The game is designed for hospitals, nursing schools, simulation centers, and disaster preparedness teams.

1

Scenario Input

Teams receive an infectious disease disaster situation with evolving constraints.

2

Ward-Level Decisions

Players allocate nurses, manage ward conversion, and respond to changing risks.

3

Outcome Tracking

Actions influence workload, infection spread, staffing capacity, and resilience indicators.

4

Structured Debriefing

Teams review AI-supported feedback to improve preparedness and decision-making.
