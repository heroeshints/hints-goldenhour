The challenge

Procedure-based training cannot fully reproduce the complexity of a hospital crisis.

Healthcare teams need to practice under uncertainty and see how delayed decisions, resource conflicts, fragmented information, and changing priorities shape hospital-wide outcomes over time.

🏥

Uncertain Operating Conditions

Training must reflect patient surges, bed shortages, infection-control pressure, staffing strain, and cascading operational disruption.

🧠

System-Level Consequences

Learners need to understand how one local decision can alter patient flow, staffing, continuity, and institutional stability.

📈

Evidence-Based Debriefing

Training should capture decision paths and connect them to visible outcomes so facilitators can review what happened and why.

Platform

A hybrid training workflow from tabletop decisions to predictive insight.

The platform integrates a reusable tabletop simulation, mobile decision logging, backend scenario computation, operational visualization, and structured debriefing into one training workflow.

01

Tabletop decision interface

Participants manage patient surges, bed capacity, staffing, infection-control measures, and ward-level priorities under time pressure.

02

Mobile decision tracking

The mobile interface records team decisions, displays current ward status, and supports scenario progression and review.

03

Scenario computation

A backend simulation engine translates decisions into changing operational conditions and supports alternative-strategy comparison.

04

Data-supported debriefing

Teams review decision sequences, operational trajectories, and alternative outcomes to identify stronger response strategies.

Integrated Training Flow

End-to-end
1. Play Teams make operational decisions using a physical board-game interface.
2. Input Decisions are entered into the mobile interface for visualization and analysis.
3. Compute The engine calculates operational consequences and long-term scenario trajectories.
4. Debrief Teams compare actual choices with alternative strategies through structured debriefing.
Mobile interface for ward status, team progress, and training results.

Experiential design

Make fragmented information and hospital-wide trade-offs visible.

Learners practice difficult decisions involving patient prioritization, bed conversion, nurse allocation, infection control, and continuity planning. The experience is designed to support team discussion, rapid decision-making, coordinated response, and structured reflection.

Decision analytics

Connect decision patterns, operational outcomes, and debriefing.

The platform captures individual and team decisions, reconstructs the sequence of actions, and links them to operational outcomes. Facilitators can use performance trends and competency indicators to guide structured reflection and follow-up training.
